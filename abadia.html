Inside the archive

The archive also includes a room atlas, a stroke-by-stroke reconstruction of the manuscript, and an explorer for the game’s seven days.

The atlas lets you navigate the abbey as a set of connected rooms. The seven-day explorer follows the schedule of the game and the characters’ movements through it. The archive also preserves recordings and magazine coverage alongside the material extracted from the game.
