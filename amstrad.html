Amstrad PC Emulator

Insert a disk, press the power switch and listen for the drive head. The cabinet itself is the interface to an 86Box-powered PC1512 or PC1640.

View on GitHub

At the machine

The drive bay opens the disk selector and the monitor badge switches displays. The PC1512 and PC1640 can each be configured with one floppy drive, two floppy drives, or a floppy drive and an HD20 hard disk.

The drive lights follow the emulated hardware, and recordings of period drives bring back the sound of a floppy seeking or the hard disk spinning up. A close-up view shows the curved glass and reflections of the CRT.
